Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

RushID ("we", "the site") is a free browser-based tool for laying out ID and passport photos for printing. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Your Photos

RushID does not have a server component that receives your images. Every step — reading the file, cropping, zooming, packing photos onto a virtual sheet, and generating the final PDF — runs as JavaScript inside your own browser. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server we operate.

Local Storage

RushID saves your last-used paper size, orientation, margins, and selected print sizes in your browser's local storage so they're remembered on your next visit. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this page.

Advertising & Cookies

This site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance. We do not control this data collection directly — it is governed by Google's own policies.

  • Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Manage your ad personalization settings: Google Ads Settings

Other Third-Party Resources

To render the page and generate PDFs, RushID loads a small number of resources from third-party content delivery networks: Google Fonts (for typefaces) and jsPDF (for PDF export), in addition to the Google AdSense script above. Requests to these services are subject to the respective providers' own privacy policies and may involve standard web request data (such as your IP address) being visible to them, the same as with any resource loaded from a CDN.

Children's Privacy

RushID is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact
