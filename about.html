About RushID

RushID is a small, free tool for one specific chore: turning a single square photo into a full sheet of correctly-sized ID, passport, and visa prints, ready to cut apart and hand to whoever needs them. It exists because getting a handful of passport-sized photos usually means a trip to a photo counter, a paid print run, and a wait — for something that's really just a crop and a print layout problem.

The tool runs entirely as JavaScript in your browser. There's no server-side upload, no account, and no processing pipeline you can't see: your photo is read locally, cropped locally, arranged on a virtual sheet locally, and exported to a PDF locally. Nothing about your image is transmitted anywhere as part of using RushID.

Why It's Free

RushID doesn't charge for use and doesn't require sign-up. The site is supported by advertising, which covers hosting and ongoing development. There is no premium tier, paywall, or usage limit.
