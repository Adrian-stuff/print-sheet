How RushID Works

RushID turns a single square photo into a full sheet of correctly-sized ID and passport prints, without ever leaving your browser. Upload a photo, choose the sizes you need — from a built-in country preset or a custom width and height — and RushID centre-crops your photo to fill each frame exactly. Every print is arranged on your chosen paper size with thin cut marks at the corners, so a pair of scissors or a paper trimmer is all you need to separate them cleanly.

Because everything runs as JavaScript in your own browser, your photo is never uploaded to a server. Cropping, layout, and PDF generation all happen locally on your device, and the finished file goes straight to your downloads folder.

Supported ID & Passport Photo Sizes

RushID ships with ready-made presets for some of the most commonly requested document photo sizes, including US Passport & Visa (2×2 in), UK & Ireland Passport (35×45 mm), Schengen Visa, India Passport & PAN Card, China Visa, Canada Passport, Japan Visa & Residence Card, Australia Passport, and South Korea Visa. Standard studio sizes like 1×1, 2×2, 2×3, wallet (2.5×3.5), 3×4, 3.5×4.5, 4×6, and 5×7 inches are built in as well, and you can add any custom width, height, and quantity on top of those.

These presets are provided for convenience and reference only — document photo requirements change over time, so always confirm the current spec with the passport office, embassy, or agency you're submitting to before printing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
No. RushID processes everything — cropping, layout, and PDF export — locally in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device.
Can I print photos of more than one person on the same sheet?
Yes. Upload multiple photos and assign each size its own quantity per photo, so you can mix several people or portraits onto one print sheet.
What paper sizes does RushID support?
Letter, Legal, A4, A5, 4R, 5R, and 8R, in either portrait or landscape orientation, with adjustable margins and cut gaps.
Is RushID free to use?
Yes, RushID is free with no account or sign-up required. The site is supported by advertising.
